# Consolidated requirements
Lab 6 supplies the normative feature IDs and full acceptance criteria. Lab 7 adds design allocations and concrete checks for the selected desktop architecture. The prototype assumes one currency and one shared installation. Each row describes a future acceptance check.

| Lab 6 IDs | Required behavior and acceptance check | Responsible design element |
|---|---|---|
| FR-001–003, 017 | Sign in with email and password. Deny invalid credentials. Only an administrator edits group details or membership. Deny an unrelated account access to the group. Removing a member blocks new participation and preserves prior obligations. | Session, membership and scoped queries |
| FR-004–006 including subrules | Capture payer, creator, participants, amount, date, description, split method and optional receipt. Reject blank, nonnumeric, nonpositive or overprecision amounts. Check each percentage is 0–100 and the sum is exactly 100. Show the actual total on error. Invalid entries save nothing. | Expense service and split calculator |
| FR-005C/D | Allocate whole cents by largest remainder, breaking ties by ascending user ID. For $10 shared equally by users 1, 2 and 3, expect $3.34, $3.33 and $3.33. A 0% participant owes zero. | ExpenseShare |
| FR-007/A | On creation, notify current group members except the creator. For a direct expense notify the distinct payer and participants except the creator. Inspect each account’s in-app inbox. | Notification repository |
| FR-008/A/B, 009 | Group administrator decides even on their own expenses. For direct expenses collect approvals from all involved users except the creator. One rejection ends review and requires a reason visible with the record to authorized users. Pending and rejected records contribute zero. Retry final approval and verify identical balances. | Approval service and posting transaction |
| FR-010–012 including payment subrules | Report directional debt by pair and context, including zero. Record an external partial or full settlement. Reject invalid amounts using the expense precision rules, or amounts above the current debt. On a $6 debt, $2 leaves $4, a retry leaves $4, and a new $5 payment fails. | Payment service and balance query |
| FR-013/A, 014 | Provide basic expense, balance and payment summaries for the selected user or group. Check pending expenses appear with their status while balance totals include only posted obligations and settlements. Date-range and custom reports are optional extensions. | Scoped report queries |
| FR-015–016, FR-MVP-001–004 | Deliver a design for the Swing course prototype. Keep primary web and Android/iOS clients in full-product scope. Preserve the Lab 6 requirements inventory. | Deployment plan and source specification |

**Quality requirements (NFR-001–008).** Authorize reads and writes using the signed-in identity. Use BigDecimal for money, keep validation and finance outside Swing, and derive reports from committed postings. Show specific field errors and rejection reasons. Link the domain design and future JUnit 5 checks to the IDs above. Prototype limits and deferred decisions include multi-device deployment, full-product role granularity, receipt policies, custom reports and rejected-expense resubmission. See the [Lab 6 specification](https://github.com/mhamida2/SplitSmart-Lab6/blob/main/specs/001-splitsmart/spec.md).
